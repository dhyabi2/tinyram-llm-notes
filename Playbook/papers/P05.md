Threat to novelty: none to the K-lock-step idea itself (known). The surviving gap: nobody evaluates K small (2-8) lock-step on CPU/NVMe with a laptop cache.

Memory check: core idea (more tokens per expert read amortizes weight transfer; "expert explosion" limits it) is well known from FlexGen-style offloading through MoE-Lightning, MoE-Gen, Lynx. Closest: MoE-Gen and the energy paper. Our variant differs in regime: tiny K, disk-bound CPU, per-user speed matters, and only one user exists. The regime difference matters, and it makes the idea weaker rather than stronger.

Verdict #16: novelty = known. Published evidence for speedup in our regime = no (every headline is GPU, offline, large batch, model in host RAM; the one paper that tests SSD (P-0103) is about energy and shows saturation at batch 16-64). Our measurement: 125B aggregate gain 1.07x (K=8), 1.34x (K=32), 2.72x (K=128) at per-user 0.04x of single-user speed (C.md, formula-based; real K>3 prompts not measured). Cannot apply to us: 10.3x, 8-31x, 10x, 2.0-2.1x (batch hundreds, GPU, big host RAM).

## Cross-family findings (most important)

1. P-0113 is mis-sourced: the llama.cpp issue holds none of the quoted numbers. The numbers come from a Reddit post (mirror: prismix.dev) about a custom engine on a 68 GB 512-expert top-10 model; direct-io + prefetch + warm cache reached ~20 tok/s on an RTX 3060 with 16 GB RAM. This is the closest public precedent to our target model class; treat as a competitor claim, unverified (no code).
2. Edge0's 20.4 tok/s is not an SSD-bound result (prerouter-off 19.9 on the same machine; reads <= 12% of step time; top-4 narrowing; quality loss). The honest SSD-bound numbers are 3.3-8.6 tok/s on a 16 GB machine.
3. Paging the Experts does bundle gate/up/down per expert (catalog said no layout found), so "repack into one run" is already in use.
4. Large-batch headlines in #16/#17 (10.3x, 8-31x, 85x) are GPU/offline/host-RAM and cannot transfer; each paper's own tables are far lower (MoE-Lightning 1.2-2.1x vs FlexGen; Klotski 2.23x vs FlexGen; ExpertFlow 1.9-2.1x vs LRU cache, token scheduler alone 1.03-1.17x).
5. BigMoeOnEdge's own doc shows F_NOCACHE is +56% on a slow external volume and nearly flat on a fast internal SSD, consistent with our result that read mode alone is small.

Unverified / not opened: Lynx full text, MoE-Infinity numbers, PowerInfer-2 details, fMoE, SeqMoE, CoMoE, SMoE, the Flash-MoE primary repo, the HN io_uring comment, Janas repo/wiki, the letsdatascience article itself, Jetson dev.to write-up.
